## Project Overview

Eleanor's Boutique is an e-commerce platform that allows users to browse collections of clothing, add items to their shopping cart, and checkout with their selected products. The site has various categories (Tops, Bottoms, Jewelry, Shoes) where users can explore different items. Additionally, users can apply discounts to certain products, view the total of their cart, and proceed to checkout.

## Features Overview
*User Interaction*

**Browsing Collections:** Users can browse clothing collections by clicking on categories such as "Tops", "Bottoms", "Shoes", and "Jewelry". Each category will display a list of products with their names and prices.

**Adding Items to Cart:** Clicking the "Add to Cart" button next to an item will add that item to the user's shopping cart. Users can view and manage the cart by clicking the cart icon at the top of the page.

**Checkout:** After adding items to the cart, users can proceed to checkout, where they will be prompted to provide shipping and payment information. A summary of the total price is shown before submission.

**Order Confirmation:** Once the order is placed, the user will receive an order confirmation page with a "Thank You" message and a summary of their order.

*Features*

**Product Browsing:** Browse different collections (Tops, Bottoms, Shoes, Jewelry).
**Shopping Cart:** Add items to the cart and view the total cost.
**Checkout Process:** Users can enter shipping details and process payments.
**Discounts:** Apply a discount to specific product categories.
**Responsive Design:** The site is mobile-friendly and adapts to various screen sizes.
